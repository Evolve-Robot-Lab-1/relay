Privacy

Relay helps you write clearer messages inside websites you already use. You stay in control of every draft.

What Relay reads

  • Focused draft text and short instructions only after you open Relay and choose Improve or Create draft.
  • Optional deliberate selection and a limited nearby excerpt (for example recent visible chat messages).
  • Field labels or placeholders that help Relay understand what the field is asking.

What Relay does not do

  • Does not press Send or submit forms for you.
  • Does not continuously monitor pages or read your browsing history.
  • Does not save quick-message or focused compose content by default.
  • Does not use password, payment, identity, contact, address, or short factual fields.

What is stored

  • One anonymous installation ID in the browser for rate limiting.
  • Message-free product events (panel opened, draft generated, inserted, errors) with broad page category only.
  • Waitlist email and cohort preference if you join the waitlist.
